# Step 4: Log and Reflection

## 1. What I learned from the AI feedback

The AI helped me understand that I should check whether the dataset actually contains the information needed to answer a question. My original question about sweater prices cannot be answered because the dataset does not contain any sweater information.

## 2. What the data can tell me

The dataset contains prices of four items across 12 dates in two cities. I can compare prices of the same item over different dates and calculate how much the prices have changed.

## 3. What the data cannot tell me

The dataset cannot explain why prices increased or whether everyday items are affordable for families. It does not contain information about people's income or household spending. It also covers only four items and two cities, so it cannot represent every market.

## 4. My reflection

I learned that predictions and assumptions should be checked against actual data. I also learned that even if a question sounds useful, I must first check whether the required information is available in the dataset.
